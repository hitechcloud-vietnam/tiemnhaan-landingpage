Áo dài
amid the scent of tea

Áo dài rental, a private studio room, photographers and makeup artists, plus 6 photo corners around the shop. Decor sets change for Tết, Mid-Autumn, Valentine's and Christmas.

Áo dài Studio

An áo dài for every moment

Graduation photos, Tết portraits, couple shoots, family photos, or simply capturing a beautiful day at Nhà An.

Áo dài rental

Traditional, modern, couple and children's áo dài in many colors and sizes. Conical hats, fans and flower baskets included.

Private photo studio

A studio room with photo lighting, backdrops and seasonal decor sets, booked by time slot.

Photographer & makeup

Add a photographer and makeup artist, plus hair styling if needed. Photos are color-graded and sent via Zalo or Google Drive.

Tea & photo combo

After your shoot, stay for oolong tea and cakes, with a private changing room and storage for your belongings.
