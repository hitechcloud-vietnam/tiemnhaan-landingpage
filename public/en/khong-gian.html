Work & study spaces

The deadline hub for young people

Work flows smoothly, mind at ease. Four separate zones for four moods: team meetings, cozy time, focused deadline work and chatting with friends.

Meetings · Meeting Room

Smooth-Flow Meeting Room

A private glass room for 6–10 people, soundproofed from the common area, with a presentation screen, whiteboard and markers. Great for team meetings, group projects, interviews and practice presentations. Hourly booking available.

  • 6–10 seats, private glass room
  • HDMI presentation screen
  • Whiteboard, markers, sticky notes
  • Book by time slot
  • Tea and pastries served at your table

Best for: Team meetings · Group projects · Interviews

Book this zone
Cozy · Cozy Corner

Nhà An Cozy Corner

Sofas and armchairs by the window, soft warm lights, light blankets and a small bookshelf. For reading, journaling, meeting loved ones or simply sitting alone with a cup of tea.

  • Sofas, armchairs, light blankets
  • Soft warm lights, quiet music
  • Bookshelf for in-shop reading
  • Sockets next to seats
  • Private space, little foot traffic

Best for: Reading · Dates · Me-time

Book this zone
Deadline · Focus Zone

Deadline Room

A work and study area kept separate from the chatting area. Single and double desks with dividers, desk lamps, power outlets and charging ports at every seat, plus high-speed wifi. Quiet-hours rules: speak softly, silence your phone, take calls outside. Open 24/7, all night long.

  • Separate quiet zone
  • Desks with dividers and lamps
  • Sockets and charging ports at every seat
  • High-speed wifi
  • Open 24/7, all night long

Best for: Deadlines · Exam prep · Remote work

Book this zone
Lively · Social Zone

Chatter Courtyard

Long tables and a leafy front yard where you can chat freely. Board games and livelier music, perfect for group study, class reunions, small birthdays or simply long conversations.

  • Long tables for 8–12 people
  • Free board games
  • Leafy front yard
  • Upbeat music, laugh freely
  • Small birthday decorations available

Best for: Friends · Study groups · Small birthdays

Amenities

Everything for a fully productive day

High-speed wifi

A dedicated connection for the work zones, enough for online meetings and big downloads.

Sockets at every seat

Power outlets and USB charging ports at every table, no fighting over them.

Open 24/7

Day or night, there's always a seat, with no time limit.

Quick printing

Print and scan documents right at the counter for urgent deadlines.

Order at your table

Scan the code to order tea, pastries or vegetarian rice without leaving your seat.

Quiet hours

The Deadline zone is always quiet, with clear speak-softly rules.

House rules

So everyone feels comfortable

  1. Deadline zone: speak softly, silence your phone, step outside for calls.
  2. Meeting Room: book ahead, arrive on time, leave the room tidy.
  3. Chatter Courtyard: chat and laugh freely, keep the volume moderate after 21:00.
  4. All zones: keep tables clean, bring your own cup to live green with Nhà An.
