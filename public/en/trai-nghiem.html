Experiences

Not just a cup of tea, but a place to come home to

Four separate work and study zones, weekend workshops, small parties and a members' club: Nhà An is the place you'll want to come back to every day.

Spaces

Four corners, four moods

See details of the 4 work & study zones and book a spot on our page Spaces.

Smooth-Flow Meeting Room

A small meeting room for teams, projects and interviews. Meet up, and work flows smoothly.

Nhà An Cozy Corner

Soft sofas, warm lights, a hot cup of tea. Cozy for reading alone or for a date.

Deadline Room

A separate quiet zone with single desks, a socket at every seat and strong wifi. Sit down and get in the zone.

Chatter Courtyard

Long tables, board games, plenty of laughter. A spot for friend groups and fun study sessions.

Parties & gifts

Let Nhà An take care of the sweet part

Birthday cakes

Cream cakes, cheesecakes and mille crêpes made to order. Order 2 days ahead.

Tea & pastry gift boxes

Bagged oolong tea with macarons and cookies, perfect as gifts or Tết presents.

Office tea parties

Afternoon tea sets delivered to your meetings and internal events.

Small in-shop parties

Birthdays, class reunions, farewell parties of up to 30 guests, with decorations.
