About the shop

When does Tiệm Nhà An open?

5 branches (Binh Thanh and Tan Binh in Ho Chi Minh City; central Da Nang, Hoi An Old Town and Duy Xuyen) are being completed. Opening dates and exact addresses will be announced on our website, on Zalo and to everyone who has signed up for updates.

Is the food at the shop entirely vegetarian?

Yes. All breakfast and lunch dishes are vegetarian, with broths made from vegetables and no MSG. Vegan dishes (no eggs, no dairy) are marked separately at the counter.

Can I choose the sugar level and type of milk?

Yes. Every drink comes with a sugar level from 0% to 100%, less or more ice, and you can swap fresh milk for oat milk.

Opening hours & booking

Is Nhà An open 24/7?

Yes. All 5 branches are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, holidays and Tết included. The kitchen serves late-night vegetarian dishes from 22:00 to 04:00.

How do I book a table?

Book on our Booking page, or call or message us on Zalo at 0865 920 041. Nhà An will call you back to confirm. We take bookings for regular tables, group tables, meeting rooms, the studio and parties.

Do I need to book in advance?

It's not required. We recommend booking ahead on weekends, during holidays and Tết, for large groups, or if you need a meeting room or the studio.

Work & study spaces

How many seating zones does Nhà An have?

Every branch has 4 zones: Smooth-Flow Meeting Room (group meetings), Nhà An Cozy Corner (cozy), Deadline Room (quiet, focused) and Chatter Courtyard (chat freely).

Is there a time limit for sitting?

No. Nhà An is open 24/7, so you can work and study comfortably day and night.

How do I book the meeting room?

The Smooth-Flow Meeting Room can be booked by time slot. Send a request via the form on the Spaces page, call or message Zalo 0865 920 041.

Is there enough wifi and power?

Every seat has a socket and USB charging port. The work zones have their own high-speed wifi connection.

Parties & gifts

Do you take orders for cakes, tea parties and gifts?

Yes. Nhà An takes orders for birthday cakes, tea & pastry gift boxes and tea sets for offices or small events. Call or message Zalo 0865 920 041, or email contact@tiemnhaan.com.

Can I hold a birthday party at the shop?

Yes. The Chatter Courtyard hosts small parties of up to 30 guests, with simple decorations. Please book at least 2 days ahead.

Careers

What salary does Nhà An offer?

Part-time: VND 30,000–35,000/hour, flexible shifts. Full-time: VND 10–15 million/month, with full social and health insurance.

Can I apply without experience?

Yes. Nhà An trains you from scratch in drink-making, baking, vegetarian cooking and service.

Photography & áo dài

Do you rent out áo dài?

Yes. Nhà An rents traditional, modern, couple and children's áo dài, with conical hats, fans and flower baskets. You can also book a photographer and makeup artist.

Is there a fee to take photos at the photo corners?

No. The photo corners in the shop are for Nhà An's guests. The private studio and áo dài packages must be booked in advance; please contact us for a quote.
