Beach town, lantern town,
a little peace

A house near My Khe Beach, a yellow house in the heart of Hoi An's old town. In Central Vietnam, Nhà An brews lotus tea, cooks vegetarian Mi Quang and lights lanterns every evening.

Taste of Central Vietnam

Rich flavors of Quang land

Vegetarian dishes with the soul of Central Vietnam, only at our two branches in Da Nang and Hoi An.

Specialty

Vegetarian Mi Quang

Vegetarian Quang Noodles

Turmeric-yellow noodles, mushrooms, roasted peanuts, crispy grilled sesame rice crackers. Photo for illustration.

Central-style vegetarian bánh xèo

Central-style Crispy Pancake

Small pancakes cooked in clay molds, filled with bean sprouts and mushrooms, wrapped in wild greens and dipped in bean sauce.

Only in Hoi An

Hoi An lotus tea

Hoi An Lotus Tea

Lotus-scented oolong steeped in a Thanh Ha ceramic pot, sipped by lantern light.

Only in Da Nang

Blue sea oolong

Sea Breeze Cold-brew Oolong

Fragrant cold-brew oolong with Quang Nam passion fruit and Sa Huynh sea salt.

Tra Que ginger tea

Ginger Tea with Tra Que Herbs

Ginger, lemongrass and basil from Tra Que herb village, warming on rainy Central Vietnam days.

Cam Nam corn sweet soup

Cam Nam Sweet Corn Pudding

Cam Nam sticky corn cooked in coconut milk, a familiar Hoi An dessert.

Central Vietnam through Nhà An's eyes

Shimmering old town nights

Da Nang

Beach city

Morning swim at My Khe Beach, a bowl of vegetarian Mi Quang at Nhà An, then an afternoon on the balcony with cold-brew oolong, watching the sunset over the Han River.

Hoi An, Quang Nam

Lantern street

A yellow house in the old town. At night we light lanterns, brew lotus tea and float flower lanterns on the Hoai River with our guests.
