Nhà An is hiring for 4 branches: VND 30,000–35,000/hour and 10–15 million/month

Baristas, servers, cashiers, bakers, vegetarian kitchen assistants. No experience needed, full training, full social & health insurance for full-time staff.

To prepare for 4 branches in Saigon and Da Nang – Quang Nam, Tiệm Nhà An is hiring for many part-time and full-time positions.

Part-time staff earn VND 30,000–35,000/hour with flexible shifts, great for students. Full-time staff earn VND 10–15 million/month, with an official labor contract and full social & health insurance.

Nhà An doesn't require experience. You'll be trained in drink-making, baking, vegetarian cooking and service, with a path to shift lead and store manager.

Apply right on the Careers page, send your CV to contact@tiemnhaan.com or message Zalo 0865 920 041.
