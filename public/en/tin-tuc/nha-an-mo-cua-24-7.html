Tiệm Nhà An is open 24/7: the lights stay on for your all-night deadlines

All 5 branches are open 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, holidays and Tết included. Late-night vegetarian dishes and quiet zones for night owls.

Many young people work and study at their own rhythm: some focus best in the early morning, others only hit their stride after midnight. That's why Tiệm Nhà An decided to open 24/7 at all 5 branches.

At night, the Deadline Room stays quiet, with power outlets, desk lamps and strong wifi. The tea bar serves all night, and the kitchen offers late-night vegetarian dishes from 22:00 to 04:00.

Nhà An is staffed around the clock and stays open through holidays and Tết, so anyone far from home always has a place to come back to.
