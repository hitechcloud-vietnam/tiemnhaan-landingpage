Tiệm Nhà An is opening 5 branches in Saigon and Da Nang – Quang Nam

Binh Thanh and Tan Binh in Saigon; central Da Nang, Hoi An Old Town and Duy Xuyen by the Thu Bon River: 5 homes now being completed.

After a long period of preparation, Tiệm Nhà An officially announces plans to open its first 5 branches: Nhà An Binh Thanh, Nhà An Tan Binh, Nhà An Da Nang, Nhà An Hoi An and Nhà An Duy Xuyen.

Each branch carries the character of its own land. Binh Thanh is a townhouse with a lotus courtyard amid the high-rises, while Tan Binh is an old-Saigon tiled tea house near the airport. In Central Vietnam, there's a balcony overlooking the Han River and Dragon Bridge, a lantern-lit yellow house in Hoi An Old Town and a garden house by the Thu Bon River in Duy Xuyen.

What all 5 homes share is a dedicated work and study space with 4 zones: Smooth-Flow Meeting Room, Nhà An Cozy Corner, Deadline Room and Chatter Courtyard.

Exact addresses and opening dates will be announced on Nhà An's website and Zalo. Sign up for news to be the first to know and get opening offers.
