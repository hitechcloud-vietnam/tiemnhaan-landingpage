Signature drink

Milk oolong with cloud cream

Wood-fired Bao Loc oolong, cold-brewed for 12 hours, topped with fresh cream whipped light as a cloud. The first cup you should try at Nhà An.

  1. 01
    Picking

    One bud and two leaves, hand-picked in the early morning.

  2. 02

    Withered, tossed for aroma, hand-rolled into pearls.

  3. 03
    Wood-firing

    Longan-wood fire 3 times, a night apart each time.

    Brewing

    Hot-brewed at 92°C or cold-brewed for 12 hours.

Quick questions

Frequently asked questions

When does Tiệm Nhà An open?

4 branches (2 in Ho Chi Minh City, 2 in Da Nang – Quang Nam) are being finished. Opening dates and exact addresses will be announced on our website, on Zalo and to everyone who signs up for news.

Is the food at the shop entirely vegetarian?

Yes. All breakfast and lunch dishes are vegetarian, with broths made from vegetables and no MSG. Vegan dishes (no eggs, no dairy) are marked separately at the counter.

Can I choose the sugar level and type of milk?

Yes. Every drink comes with a sugar level from 0% to 100%, less or more ice, and you can swap fresh milk for oat milk.

Is there seating for working and studying?

Yes. Every branch has a quiet work corner with a socket at each table and strong wifi, plus a long-table area for friend groups.

What salary does Nhà An offer?

Part-time: VND 30,000–35,000/hour, flexible shifts. Full-time: VND 10–15 million/month, with full social and health insurance. Send your CV to contact@tiemnhaan.com or message Zalo 0865 920 041.

Do you take orders for cakes, tea parties and gifts?

Yes. Nhà An takes orders for birthday cakes, tea & pastry gift boxes and tea sets for offices or small events. Call or message Zalo 0865 920 041, or email contact@tiemnhaan.com.
