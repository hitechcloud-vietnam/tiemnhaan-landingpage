About this branch

A yellow house on the lantern street

Nhà An Hoi An is a mossy yellow house in the middle of the Old Town, with yin-yang tiled roofs and a small courtyard skylight. Every evening, the shop lights its lanterns, brews lotus tea and invites guests to release floating lanterns on the Hoai River. This is also the branch with our largest áo dài studio, for photos with lanterns and the Old Town.
