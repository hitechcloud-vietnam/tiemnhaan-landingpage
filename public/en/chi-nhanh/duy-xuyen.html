About this branch

By the Thu Bon River, near My Son Sanctuary

Nhà An Duy Xuyen is a peaceful garden house by the Thu Bon River, on the road from Hoi An to My Son Sanctuary. Here you'll find rice fields, the Ma Chau silk village and sunsets over the river. This branch is for anyone who wants to escape the city, work at a slower pace and enjoy a countryside vegetarian meal.

  • Riverside garden house
  • Near My Son
  • Ma Chau silk
  • Open 24/7

Highlights

Only in Duy Xuyen

Riverside garden house

Garden tables overlooking the Thu Bon River and the rice fields.

My Son rest stop

A resting spot for travelers on their way to My Son Sanctuary.

Ma Chau silk corner

A display of handwoven silk, with silk áo dài photo shoots amid the countryside.

Must-try dishes

Flavors unique to Duy Xuyen

Garden ginger tea

Ginger, lemongrass and fragrant herbs from the garden, warming on rainy afternoons.

Countryside vegetarian rice

Brown rice, garden vegetables, silken tofu and vegetable soup.

Duy Xuyen sweet corn soup

Sticky corn from the banks of the Thu Bon River, cooked in coconut milk.
