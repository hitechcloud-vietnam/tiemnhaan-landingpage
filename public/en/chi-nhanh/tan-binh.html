About this branch

Old-Saigon patterned tiles, near the airport

Nhà An Tan Binh carries the spirit of old Saigon: patterned cement tiles, wooden windows, ceiling fans and cups of phin coffee. Near Tan Son Nhat Airport, it's a familiar meeting spot for office workers, business travelers who need a place to work before their flight, and friends gathering after work.

  • Old-Saigon patterned tiles
  • Near the airport
  • Chatter Courtyard
  • Open 24/7

Highlights

Only in Tan Binh

Waiting for a night flight

Open 24/7, with luggage storage and power outlets for travelers waiting on their flights.

Spacious Chatter Courtyard

Long tables, board games and upbeat music for friend groups and office teams.

Old Saigon corner

Patterned tiles, a Vespa and a polaroid wall for nostalgic photos.

Must-try dishes

Flavors unique to Tan Binh

Salted cream phin coffee

Bold Robusta phin coffee with a lightly salty cream topping.

Kumquat & salted plum tea

The classic sidewalk iced tea, upgraded with delicate oolong.

Street-cart corn sticky rice

Corn and mung bean sticky rice with vegetarian scallion oil, just like the sticky rice stall at the alley corner.
