About this branch

A green home amid the high-rises

Nhà An's first branch is in Binh Thanh, home to some of Saigon's liveliest office and student life. Among the high-rises, Nhà An Binh Thanh is a leafy townhouse with a lotus-filled front yard, all 4 work zones and a small áo dài photo studio.

  • Lotus corner
  • Deadline Room
  • Áo dài Studio
  • Open 24/7

Highlights

Only in Binh Thanh

Deadline hub

The largest quiet zone in our network, serving office workers and students all night long.

Green lotus courtyard

A front yard with lotus and greenery, the prettiest photo spot in the morning.

10-seat meeting room

The Smooth-Flow Meeting Room has a screen and whiteboard, bookable by the hour.

Must-try dishes

Flavors unique to Binh Thanh

Oolong bạc xỉu

Saigon bạc xỉu with an extra shot of wood-roasted oolong.

Cloud cream milk oolong

Nhà An's signature drink, best enjoyed cold.

Vegetarian Phnom Penh noodle soup

A light, naturally sweet radish broth for breakfast.
